# Feedback from Ballot CH UMZH Connect DSTU 1

## General information
The DSTU 1 informative ballot ([IG v1.0.0-ballot](https://fhir.ch/ig/ch-umzh-connect/1.0.0-ballot/index.html)) was held from August 7 to September 30, 2026. It was the first ballot of CH UMZH Connect. The annual Projectathon took place from September 22-23, 2026.

Votes cast: 14 (YES: 12, NO: 2, ABSTAIN: 0). The two negative votes came with the following justification:
* **Will Schaefermeier (Epic)**: major blockers are changing `Task.owner` and `Task.focus` during the workflow ([#116](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/116), [#118](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/118)) and the missing placer-initiated cancellation ([#119](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/119)). Minor clarifications: UPDATE instead of PATCH ([#117](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/117)), notifications ([#121](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/121)), role of Consent and of the custom context-centric authorization ([#122](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/122), [#123](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/123)).
* **Fabian Schwarzer (Cistec AG)**: the Clinical Order Workflow (COW) IG is still STU 1 and not stable enough as a basis for cantonal implementations; the national recommendation is based on CH ORF, which some vendors have partially implemented and which uses a different logic. A nationwide discussion on the exchange procedure is requested. This is covered by [#126](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/126) (Walter Wellauer, Cistec AG).

During the ballot, 28 issues were reported for CH UMZH Connect (see [DSTU 1 Ballot](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22DSTU%201%20Ballot%22)). One issue ([#91](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/91)) was filed shortly before the ballot period and is included. No related issues were found in the hl7ch repositories (CH Core, CH Term, CH eTOC, CH ORF); [#126](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/issues/126) however asks for changes that affect CH ORF, CH eTOC and CH RAD-Order.

Main topics:
* **Workflow design** (Task.owner as permission, changing Task.focus, PATCH, cancellation, notifications): mostly from Epic, partly with a negative vote. These touch the core of the IG and need a decision in the working group.
* **Authorization** (`fhirContext`, context-centric authorization, Consent, counter-party check): requests for rationale and consistency of the security model.
* **Alignment with Swiss IGs and COW maturity** (CH ORF / CH eTOC): negative vote from Cistec, needs a discussion beyond this IG.
* **CapabilityStatement and interactions**: search parameters and `_include` handling.
* **Editorial and examples**: most of them are already fixed in [PR #112](https://github.com/umzhconnect/umzhconnect-ig/pull/112) (merged October 1, 2026).

Effort estimate:
* **Low**: editorial or a single, local change in FSH, with no discussion needed
* **Medium**: profile, API or documentation change that affects examples or implementations (sandbox), or that needs a decision in the working group
* **High**: new design or mapping work, or input from external organizations needed
